Confidentiality

Complaints relating to the performance of one of our customers will typically be forwarded to that customer for review and response. For all other complaints, confidentiality will be maintained, and information will not be disclosed to a third party without the complainant's consent.

This process also applies when Lloyds Bureau receives an appeal, whether it relates to a complaint decision or a certification decision, including suspension or withdrawal.
